/ why noltec

Enterprise IT is rarely broken. It's just left half-finished.

Most environments don't fail because of bad tools. They fail because nobody owned the last 20%. Migrations stall. Policies drift. Endpoints diverge. Scripts live on one technician's desktop. I finish the last mile. Then I keep it maintained.

01

Half-migrated identity

ADFS still answering SAML. Entra ID running alongside. Conditional access blocked by legacy claims.

02

Drifting endpoints

Intune fighting Group Policy. SCCM still pushing legacy installs. Defender baselines never enforced.

03

Audited, not hardened

ASR rules untuned. Certificates near expiry. Conditional access bypassed by old service accounts.

04

Manual everything

The same console clicks every week. Scripts only one person can read. Runbooks nobody has touched.
